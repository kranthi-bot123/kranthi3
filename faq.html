Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

How much do your services cost?

Advice and site visits are free. Before you commit, we share a written breakdown of every cost: the property price, registration, stamp duty and any service fee. You always know what you pay and why.

How fast will you reply?

Very fast. Call us and we pick up any time. On WhatsApp or the contact form, we reply within minutes during working hours.

What are your working hours?

Our office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. You can still call or WhatsApp us 24/7, and we will get back to you quickly.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

We cover all of Hyderabad, including Gachibowli, HITEC City, Kokapet, Kondapur, Banjara Hills, Kompally and Uppal. If your locality is not on our list, call us and we will tell you what is available.

How do I book a site visit?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp or fill in the contact form. Tell us your budget and area. We shortlist options, confirm a time with you and take you to see them. Site visits are free.

Are your properties verified and RERA-approved?

We shortlist RERA-registered projects and check ownership and approvals before we show a property. We share the registration details and key documents with you so you can verify them yourself.

Can you help with a home loan?

Yes. We connect you with trusted banks and guide you on documents, eligibility and the process. The loan decision and terms always come from the bank.

How do I pay for a property?

Pay only through proper banking channels, such as bank transfer or cheque, directly to the builder or seller. Always get a receipt. We explain each payment stage so there are no surprises.
