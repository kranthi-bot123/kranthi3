FAQ

Your Questions, Answered Clearly

Costs, safety, loans and booking. Straight answers before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

CostDo you charge buyers for site visits or shortlisting?

No. Site visits, pickup and drop, and your verified shortlist are free. If any fee applies to your purchase, it is written on your cost sheet before you book. Nothing is added later.

CostAre there hidden charges on the property price?

No. Before booking you get a written cost sheet for the exact unit: base price, development and amenity charges, GST where applicable, and a stamp duty and registration estimate. If a number changes, we explain why.

Response timeHow fast will you respond?

Calls are answered 24/7. Missed calls are returned within 15 minutes. Your verified shortlist is shared on WhatsApp within 24 hours, and most site visits happen within 2 to 3 days.

VerificationHow do you check that a property is legally safe?

Every project is checked for RERA registration and HMDA or DTCP approval, and title documents are reviewed with partner advocates before we show it to you. We share the RERA number so you can verify it yourself on the TS-RERA website.

PaymentCan you help me get a home loan, and how do payments work?

Yes. We work with leading banks and guide you from loan eligibility to disbursement. Payments are made directly to the developer or seller as per the written payment schedule. Never pay cash without a receipt.

HoursWhat are your working hours?

Phone and WhatsApp are open 24/7, including weekends and holidays. Site visits run every day by appointment.

CoverageWhich areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

All of Hyderabad, with a focus on growth corridors such as Gachibowli, Kokapet, Tellapur, Kollur, Shamshabad, Adibatla and Shadnagar. If your area is not listed, call us. We likely have verified options there.

BookingHow do I book a site visit?

Call or WhatsApp +91 74837 36358 with your budget and preferred area. We shortlist verified options, fix a time that suits you and arrange pickup. Booking takes about two minutes.
